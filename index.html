Understanding Cross-Chargeability

Under INA § 202(b), an immigrant applicant can be "charged" to their spouse's country of birth if it prevents family separation.

Example: If the primary applicant was born in India (where EB-2 has a 12+ year wait), but their spouse was born in Germany or UK (Rest of World - ROW), the primary applicant can claim ROW chargeability and may use the spouse's chargeability if the legal requirements are satisfied; the timing still depends on the applicable category and cutoff.

Check the "Spousal Cross-Chargeability" option on the calculator to see how drastically your timeline accelerates!
